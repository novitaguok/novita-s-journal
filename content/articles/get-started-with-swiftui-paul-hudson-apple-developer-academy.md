## 🛠️ Technical Insights: Declarative SwiftUI vs. Imperative UIKit

Technically speaking, SwiftUI brings a declarative approach compared to the imperative style of UIKit (similar to Android's transition from XML to Jetpack Compose). Having spent time coding in UIKit, switching to SwiftUI feels like a game-changer for development speed — it drastically cuts down boilerplate code and handles complex state management effortlessly.
